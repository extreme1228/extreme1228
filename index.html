What I do

I work on scaling LLM-based agents with reinforcement learning — building the data pipelines, sampling strategies, and training systems that let computer-use agents learn from verifiable online interaction.

This site is a living notebook: a place to share ideas, paper notes, and research progress.

Highlights

  • Ph.D. in Computer Science at Tsinghua University (KEG Lab).
  • First author of ScaleCUA — open-source SOTA on OSWorld (68.7%) and ScienceBoard (54.0%).
  • One year at Zhipu AI on LLM post-training and GUI Agent RL.
  • Publications at NeurIPS 2024, ACL 2026 Long Papers, and preprints on arXiv.

Research

Current directions

Computer-Use Agents

Verifiable task synthesis and online RL for agents that perceive and act on real GUIs, scaling to open-source SOTA on OSWorld and ScienceBoard.

Agentic RL Systems

Asynchronous, train–inference-decoupled RL frameworks with multi-task, multi-turn training and high-concurrency rollout over Dockerized environments.

Auto Post-Training

Multi-agent systems that automate the agent post-training loop end-to-end: data collection, environment adaptation, task construction, rollout, and quality auditing.

Experience

Industry research

Zhipu AI (智谱) — Research Intern

Feb 2025 – Present

LLM post-training & GUI / computer-use agent RL · KEG collaboration

  • Built core agentic-RL infrastructure: AgentRL, a from-scratch, highly asynchronous, train–inference-decoupled online RL framework with Dockerized multi-environment deployment and high-concurrency rollout for multi-task, multi-turn training.
  • Led verifiable task synthesis and online RL for computer-use agents — the VeriGen pipeline, Frontier Sampling, and Visual Context Segmentation — reaching open-source SOTA (ScaleCUA, first author).
  • Leading Auto-Post-Train (APT) — an AI-for-AI, self-improving multi-agent system that fully automates the agent post-training pipeline: data collection, environment adaptation, task construction, rollout, quality auditing, and model training, so models keep self-improving with minimal human intervention.
